Quick answers

Siri says she can't help with that.

The first time you use a Siri phrase, iOS asks permission to run Log's shortcuts — the prompt is easy to miss. Say the phrase again and confirm, or open the app once and retry. Note that “Hey Siri, Log help” only works while Log is already open (a tappable in-app Help button arrives in build 12) — and the surest ways to capture don't need Siri at all: the Action Button, Control Center, a widget, or the capture bar.

Where do deleted entries go?

Recently Deleted (under the filter menu) keeps them for 30 days, then removes them permanently. You can restore or erase them there any time.

How does sync work?

Entries save to your device instantly and sync through your personal iCloud in the background — no account to create, nothing passes through our hands. Large videos take longer than text.

What's the fastest way to capture?

Whatever's nearest: the Action Button, Control Center's Quick Log, a lock-screen or home-screen widget, or the capture bar in the app (type, hold the mic, camera, or photo library). On Mac, ⌥⌘L from any app or the menu-bar ＋. On the watch, raise your wrist and tap ＋. Siri works too — “Hey Siri, Log a note”, then dictate.

A few Siri phrases misbehave in the beta — what should I know?

We're refining Siri for build 12; until then the buttons above are the most reliable way to capture. Specifically:

  • Avoid “Log my location” for now. Siri can mishear it (especially with a strong accent) and switch off your Find My location sharing. Log has no way to change that setting itself — it's Apple mis-hearing the phrase — but skip it until build 12, which uses a clearer phrase. If it happened to you, turn sharing back on in Find My → People.
  • “Log a note” may ask whether you mean Log or Notes — just choose Log.

How do I report a problem?

In TestFlight: take a screenshot in Log, tap its preview → Share Beta Feedback (device and build details attach automatically). Or TestFlight → Log → Send Beta Feedback. If Log ever crashes, send the crash report when TestFlight offers. Email works too: log@bobobrien.net.

Which devices does Log need?

iPhone or iPad on iOS/iPadOS 18+, Mac on macOS 15+, and/or Apple Watch on watchOS 11+ — each signed in to iCloud. The more devices, the better: syncing across them is the whole point.

Quick answers

Siri says she can't help with that.

The first time you use a Siri phrase, iOS asks permission to run Log's shortcuts — the prompt is easy to miss. Say the phrase again and confirm, or open the app once and retry. Then try “Hey Siri, Log help” to hear everything you can say.

Where do deleted entries go?

Recently Deleted (under the filter menu) keeps them for 30 days, then removes them permanently. You can restore or erase them there any time.

How does sync work?

Entries save to your device instantly and sync through your personal iCloud in the background — no account to create, nothing passes through our hands. Large videos take longer than text.
